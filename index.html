I

Summary

A brief overview of the Fresko idea

One shared cold room per 40 farms, one app, one promise — no farmer pays to start.

1The problem

  • Highland vegetables travel 6–8 hours unrefrigerated from Benguet to Manila.
  • 27–42% of fruits and vegetables are lost post-harvest, peaking at the trading post.
  • Farmers sell at ₱4–6/kg while city retail reaches ₱60/kg.

2The solution

  • Fresko Hub — modular 2-tonne solar cold room placed at the barangay or cooperative.
  • Fresko App — books shared cooling slots, predicts shelf life, matches buyers.
  • Fresko Route — consolidated refrigerated dispatch and 24-hour digital payout.

3The value proposition

  • Same harvest, ~20% more sellable kilos.
  • Payment in 24 hours instead of 7–30 days.
  • Zero capex for farmers — pay-as-you-store, deducted at settlement.

III

Proposed startup solution

The Fresko Hub: cooling, intelligence, and a buyer in one loop

01Book & harvest

Farmer reserves a cold-room slot in the app; harvest is timed to the booking.

›
02Pre-cool in 2 hrs

Produce reaches the hub within 2 hours and is pre-cooled to 4–8 °C in solar-powered chambers.

›
03Grade & predict

IoT sensors + freshness AI score shelf life and sort into A/B/C grades automatically.

›
04Match & pay

Matched to a verified buyer; consolidated reefer dispatch; e-wallet payout in 24 hours.

VII

Business model

Create value, deliver it, and capture a slice

✦1 · Create

Co-op provides land and an operator; Fresko provides the solar cold room, sensors, app, and buyer network. Capex stays on our books, so farmer cost stays at zero.

⇉2 · Deliver

Shared capacity (not ownership), consolidated refrigerated dispatch, digital settlement, and monthly loss reports for the co-op.

₱3 · Capture

Four revenue lines, all recurring, all linked to produce actually saved — so our income grows only when farmers earn.

◈Positioning

Hardware players sell boxes; software players sell listings. Fresko owns the farm-side layer where the loss actually happens.

↗Trends we ride

  • 540,000 pallet-position cold-chain gap; DA/PRDP funding for farm-side facilities.
  • Food inflation makes loss-reduction a top-of-mind LGU agenda.
  • E-grocery and food service want traceable, graded local produce.
  • Carbon and ESG reporting demand for food-loss avoidance.

⚔Competitors & our edge

  • Trading posts — no cooling; we plug in, not replace.
  • Reefer logistics (e.g. established cold 3PLs) — urban and per-trip; we are farm-side and shared.
  • Solar cold-room suppliers — hardware only; we bundle demand and payment.
  • B2B agri platforms — listings without cold assurance; we guarantee freshness.

IX

Operations plan

Install in 21 days · operate in one barangay · clone it

0M1–M3 · Phase 0 — Partnership & site readiness

Sign the MOA with the partner cooperative (land + operator), secure LGU permits and DA endorsement, run the baseline loss audit on 3 farms, recruit the hub technician.

1M4–M6 · Phase 1 — Hub 1 live + 40-farmer pilot

Install and commission hub #1, onboard 40 farmers in under 15 minutes each, launch app v1 (offline-first), run the first matched offtake cycles with vendors.

2M7–M12 · Phase 2 — 3 hubs, app GA, route partner

Clone the playbook to Atok and La Trinidad, integrate e-wallet escrow payouts, contract a refrigerated dispatch partner, publish the first verified impact report.

3Y2 · Scale — co-op-operated franchise

12 hubs on an 80/20 revenue share: the co-op runs day-to-day, Fresko supplies technology, training and demand. Target contribution ₱33k per hub per month.

✓Standard operating procedure

  • Hourly temperature logs; alerts above 9 °C
  • HACCP-aligned handling & crate hygiene
  • FEFO rotation and pre-cool within 2 hours
  • Weekly preventive maintenance; monthly audits

⌗Tech architecture

  • LoRaWAN/GSM sensors → cloud time-series store
  • Shelf-life model + rule engine for grade & routing
  • App offline-first, Filipino/Ilocano, SMS fallback
  • GCash/Maya escrow settlement API

⚠Risks & mitigation

  • Power interruptions → solar + 5 kWh battery + genset hookup
  • Low initial utilisation → anchor offtake contract with 2 vendors
  • Trust/adoption → co-op-led onboarding, free first month
